YouTube doesn't use one simple rule to choose videos. It predicts what each viewer may want to watch and enjoy, using many signals that change with the person and the situation.

01 / The process

How recommendations work

Think of it as a loop: understand the viewer, choose possible videos, observe the response, then learn from new signals.

🧭
1. Learn interests

Watch history, searches, subscriptions and feedback help indicate what you like.

→
🗂️
2. Find candidates

The system finds videos that may fit your interests and context.

→
📊
3. Rank options

It estimates which videos are relevant and likely to satisfy you.

→
▶️
4. Show videos

Recommendations appear on Home, Up Next and the Shorts feed.

→
🔁
5. Learn again

Your choices and feedback help personalize future recommendations.

This is a simplified teaching model. YouTube uses different systems and signals for different surfaces.

02 / Signals

What information can matter?

No single signal guarantees a recommendation. YouTube combines signals to estimate relevance and viewer satisfaction.

🕘

Watch history

Videos you watch, skip, or return to can help the system understand your interests.

🔎

Searches

What you search for can indicate a topic you want to learn about or explore.

👍

Feedback

Likes, dislikes, “Not interested,” and “Don't recommend channel” help guide suggestions.

⏱️

Viewer retention

Whether people keep watching and how much they watch can help show if a video holds their interest.

💬

Satisfaction

Likes alone are not the whole story. YouTube also uses satisfaction signals, including surveys.

🌍

Context & interests

Topic, device, time, language, similar viewers and competing videos can affect what is relevant.

05 / For creators

Improve the video for real viewers

There is no guaranteed trick to go viral. Focus on making a video that the intended audience wants to click and is glad they watched.

DO THIS
🎯
Choose a clear topic

Make it obvious who the video is for and what viewers will get.

🖼️
Use an honest title and thumbnail

Make the promise clear, then deliver what the packaging promises.

✂️
Respect the viewer's time

Start with value, keep the structure clear, and remove unnecessary filler.

📈
Learn from Analytics

Check impressions, click-through rate, audience retention and traffic sources together.

MYTH CHECK
“Post at one magic time.”

There is no universal best time. Audience habits vary.

“Every video must go viral immediately.”

Videos can find interested viewers over time; one weak upload does not automatically doom a channel.

“More tags always mean more views.”

Metadata can help describe content, but it cannot replace relevance and viewer satisfaction.

“The system only cares about watch time.”

It considers multiple signals, including appeal, engagement and satisfaction.
